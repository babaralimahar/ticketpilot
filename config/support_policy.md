# Northwind Shop customer support policy (synthetic)

## Damaged items
Items that arrive damaged can be refunded within 30 days of delivery, including final-sale items.
The refund is the price paid for the damaged item(s), never more than what remains refundable on
the order. Refunds above USD 50 are reviewed by a supervisor before they are issued.

## Returns (change of mind)
Unwanted items can be returned for a refund within 30 days of delivery. Final-sale items (marked
"sale" or "clearance") cannot be returned. Refunds above USD 50 are reviewed by a supervisor.

## Orders that have not arrived
If the carrier reports a parcel as lost, or it is more than 7 days past its estimated delivery
date, the order can be refunded. Before that, share the tracking status and the estimated date.

## Changing the delivery address
Addresses can be changed before the order ships. After shipping, a carrier redirect needs
supervisor approval. Delivered or cancelled orders cannot be changed.

## What we never do
We never share information about another customer's orders, never ask for passwords or full card
numbers, and never promise a refund before it has been approved.
